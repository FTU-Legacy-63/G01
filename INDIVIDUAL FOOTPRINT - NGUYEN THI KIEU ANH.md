6. ### **REMAINING LIMITATION** 

- ### **Simplified investment mechanics:** The game rulebook uses fixed equity terms and a predefined capital allocation mechanism, limiting players’ flexibility in structuring investments. The absence of additional financing mechanisms, such as leverage, also restricts the game’s ability to reflect the complexity of real-world investment decisions. Furthermore, playing three consecutive cases under the current structure may create a demanding and potentially repetitive experience for players.

- ### **Case engagement and complexity:** Although Clinova AI was developed based on real-world healthcare AI business challenges and VC/PE due diligence principles, the case may not provide sufficient complexity or engaging decision-making challenges for players. The initial emphasis on positive indicators and relatively identifiable risks may limit opportunities for deeper analysis and critical evaluation.
