### **FORMULAS/RULES/SCORING** 

### **A. Checklist Accuracy**

**Formula**

**$Accuracy\ =\ \frac{Correct\ Assessments}{Total\ Assessments}\ \times \ 100$**

* **Indicator:** Accuracy of player's business evaluation, including red flags identification  
* **Scale:** 0–100  
* **Interpretation:** Higher \= more accurately identified company strengths/weaknesses  
* **Threshold:** 85+ Excellent, 70–84 Good, 50–69 Moderate, \<50 Weak  
* **Limitation:** Correct assessments are based on the development team's predefined evaluation of each company.

### **B. Investment Return**

**Formula**

**$Final Capital = Initial Capital +  Sum  (Investment_t  x  Return_i)$**

**$Portfolio\ Return\ =\ \frac{Final\ Capital\ -\ Initial\ Capital}{Initial\ Capital}\ \times \ 100$**

| Portfolio return | Return score |
| ----- | ----- |
| ≥ 50% | 100 |
| 30–49% | 80 |
| 15–29% | 60 |
| 1–14% | 40 |
| 0 to −14% | 20 |
| \< −15% | 0 |

* **Indicator:** Financial outcome of player's investment decisions and capital allocation  
* **Scale:** 0-100  
* **Interpretation:** Higher \= better portfolio performance  
* **Return:** Determined in advance by the development team based on company characteristics and information provided to players  
* **Limitation:** Returns are simulated assumptions and do not represent real-world expected returns.

### **C. Overall Performance**

**Overall = 50% x Accuracy + 50% x ReturnScore**

* **Indicator:** Measures both the player’s analytical ability through Checklist Accuracy and the effectiveness of translating that analysis into investment decisions and capital allocation.   
* **Scale:** 0-100  
* **Interpretation:** Higher \= better performance  
* **Threshold:** 85-100 Shark (Excellent), 70-84 Tuna (Strong), 50-69 Clownfish (Average), 30-49 Pufferfish (Weak), 0-29 Minnow (Poor)  
* **Limitation:** Arbitrary weighting
