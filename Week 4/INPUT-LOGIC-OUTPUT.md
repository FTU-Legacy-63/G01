## INPUT - LOGIC - OUTPUT MAPPING

| **Input** | **Financial meaning** | **Rule / Calculation / Process** | **Output** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level designers’ scenario design | Simulated investment scenario for players to practice their due diligence | Decide on the levels’ core scenario components and assumptions: Company sector, outcome of investment (ROI), entrepreneur personality, hidden trap/gem players discover through their due diligence, core investment thesis (checklist) | Qualitative Information tabs (Entrepreneur Pitch, Q&A with Founders, Company Overview), Checklist, fixed startup ROI |
| Real-world industry data | Market/financial benchmark | Select relevant benchmark and assumptions | Industry Information tab, Financial assumptions |
| Financial assumptions | Basis for fictional company | Construct simulated financial statements through team assumptions and modifications to stay consistent with scenario design | Income statement / Balance sheet / Cash flow statement |
| Core Information Tabs (Founder’s pitch, Q&A…) | Available data meant to aid user’s decision making process | Players will process the data to perform their due diligence and form their investment decision. | Checklist answers, invested capital |
| Player checklist answers | Player's analysis | Compare with benchmark assessment | Checklist Accuracy |
| Player investment + fixed startup ROI | Investment outcome | Profit = Investment × ROI | Investment return |
| All investments + remaining capital | Portfolio | Aggregate investment outcomes | Portfolio ROI |
| Checklist Accuracy + Portfolio ROI | Overall Player’s performance | Integrated on a scale of 0 - 100, Feedback is given based on points threshold | Player Evaluation |
| Player’s investment decision + Portfolio ROI + Checklist accuracy + Player Evaluation | Data collected from player’s interaction with the game | Combined together | Investment Decision Report (main output) |
