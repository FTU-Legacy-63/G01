# ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS

1. **Company assumptions:**

- All are fictional and represent predefined business scenarios.
- Each startup has a predefined investment thesis, strengths and weaknesses.

2. **Financial assumptions:**

- Time value of money factors are not included.
- Classification thresholds are established by the development team based on relevant benchmarks or explicit game-design assumptions.

3. **Investment assumptions:**

- Each startup has a predetermined simulated ROI - which is fixed and not randomized. A high ROI corresponds to a good company and therefore a good investment.
- Uninvested capital has a 0% return.
- The amount investable is capped to max investable.

4. **Player assumptions:**

- Players are rational, which makes:
  1. The consistency between their capital allocation (and therefore their portfolio performance) and checklist score is irrelevant.
  2. The results accurately reflect the player’s performance within the simulated game environment (no punishing mechanics for random guesses).
- Players begin with a fixed amount of capital.
- The player's checklist answers are compared with predefined benchmark checklists.

5. **Evaluation assumptions:**

- Checklist Accuracy measures analytical accuracy.
- Checklist items are designed as Yes/No questions and only accept binary inputs.
- Portfolio ROI measures investment outcome and indirectly reflects capital allocation.
- Overall Performance combines these two measures.
- Fish classifications are game-design categories rather than measures of real-world investment expertise.
