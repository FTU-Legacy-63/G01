# **PROJECT PROPOSAL**

1. **Problem Statement**

*“Finance students and beginner investors often possess solid theoretical knowledge but lack practical **due diligence** skills. “*

They can be easily influenced by charismatic pitches and vanity metrics without critically cross-checking financial statements and business fundamentals. As a result, impulsive capital allocation can quickly deplete their available funds, while the risks of real-world financial markets limit their opportunities to practice hands-on investment decision-making.

2. **Target User**

Undergraduate students in Finance, Accounting, Economics, and Business Administration seeking a risk-free simulation to apply analytical thinking and make real-world investment decisions.

3. **User Task & Core Decision**

* **Role:** Venture Capitalist (VC) managing a multi-deal investment fund.  
* **Core Decision:** *"Should I invest in this company? If yes, how much capital at what valuation/equity terms?"*  
* **Workflow:** Analyze market context  Cross-examine founder (Q\&A)  Allocate limited Investigation Points (IP) to audit financials/operations  Negotiate term sheet  Track portfolio impact.  
    
4. **Desired Outcome**

* Develop a structured due diligence methodology instead of relying on intuition.  
* Distinguish between promotional narratives and true fundamentals (Unit economics, Burn rate, Cash runway).  
* Master capital allocation and risk-adjusted decision-making under resource constraints.

5. **Main Visible Output: Investment Decision Report**

| Output Component | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Investment Decision & Terms** | Invest vs. Decline; Capital allocated; Final negotiated equity (%). |
| **Financial & Risk Assessment** | Underlying risk level identified vs. Startup's true business health. |
| **Performance & Score** | Overall decision quality score based on diligence depth and deal terms. |
| **Learning Feedback** | Personalized breakdown of analytical strengths, blind spots, and red flags missed. |
| **Portfolio Status** | Updated fund cash balance, active holdings, and projected fund IRR. |

6. **Proposed Solution & Product Form**

* VentureVerse: A turn-based educational web simulation placing students in the role of a Venture Capitalist evaluating early-stage startups across various industries.  
* Core Experience: Replaces passive lectures with active, scenario-based due diligence under realistic conditions of information asymmetry and resource constraints.  
7. **Product Logic & Evaluation Workflow**

START  
  ↓  
Receive entrepreneur pitch  
  ↓  
Review basic company information  
  ↓  
Investigate company  
 ├── Financial information  
 ├── Industry information  
 └── Ask entrepreneur questions (Q\&A)  
  ↓  
Evaluate company  
  ↓  
Invest / Reject  
  ↓  
Repeat for remaining companies  
  ↓  
Portfolio results  
  ↓  
Performance \+ feedback

8. **Project Objectives**

* **Educational Objectives:**  
  * Train students to evaluate businesses using fundamentals (Unit Economics, Burn Rate, Cash Runway) rather than charismatic pitches.  
  * Develop a multi-variable investment framework balancing founder quality, market size (TAM), and financial sustainability.  
      
* **Product & Technical Objectives:**  
  * Build a complete end-to-end VC workflow (Sourcing  Due Diligence  Term Sheet  Portfolio Tracking).  
  * Implement a modular architecture that decouples game logic from startup datasets, enabling seamless addition of new industry cases.  
      
9. **Expected Contributions**

| Pillar | Core Contribution |
| ----- | ----- |
| Educational | Shifts financial education from passive theory to experiential learning, teaching rigorous due diligence and evidence-based reasoning. |
| Technical | Establishes a reusable, modular framework for scenario-based financial simulations (plug-and-play JSON datasets for startup cases). |
| Practical | Provides a zero-risk sandbox for students to practice high-stakes capital allocation, valuation, and term sheet negotiations. |
