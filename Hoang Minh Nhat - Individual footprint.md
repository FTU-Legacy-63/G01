**1\. My main responsibility**

My main responsibility was developing the financial data and analytical logic behind the investment simulation game. **2\. My concrete outputs**

* **Three-statement financial reports:** Prepared financial statements for fictional startups, including the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement, to support financial analysis within the game.  
* **Industry information:** Researched market size, growth forecasts, industry characteristics, and financial reference ranges to provide context for evaluating startup performance.  
* **Data flow and input processing:** Contributed to defining how company data and player inputs are processed and used by the game's evaluation logic.  
* **Data mapping:** Organized financial indicators, company information, and checklist answers so they could be connected to the appropriate game components.

**4\. How my work is used**

My work provides the financial information and data-processing foundation for the game. The three-statement reports allow players to examine a startup's financial position, profitability, and cash flows. Industry information provides reference points for interpreting company performance, while data mapping connects these inputs to the relevant evaluation criteria.

The data flow and input-processing logic help ensure that company information and player responses can be used by the game's evaluation system. Together, these contributions support the core gameplay process: reviewing evidence, assessing investment opportunities, making decisions, and receiving performance feedback.

**5\. Integration, testing and revision**

I structured the financial reports and industry information for integration with the game's company data and evaluation components. I also considered the consistency between the three financial statements, the assumptions used to construct the fictional companies, and the indicators used in the investment checklist.

The data and assumptions were organized to make their intended use within the game clear. Evidence of further integration, testing, and revisions will be provided through the relevant repository files and implementation records.

**6\. Remaining limitation**

The financial statements and industry reference ranges are designed for an educational simulation rather than a comprehensive representation of real businesses. Some figures and thresholds rely on development team assumptions because directly comparable industry data may be unavailable. In addition, the simplified data-processing and evaluation framework cannot capture every factor affecting real-world investment decisions or market outcomes.
